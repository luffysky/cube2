Privacy policy

Zhuan Zhuan Battle · 斯諾瑞姆工作室（SnowRealm）· Other regions · 20 November 2026 updated

Draft: written for the law that applies where you live and waiting for review by a lawyer; anything marked [TO CONFIRM] is not settled yet. The reviewed version, once published, is the one that counts.

No sign-up and no email are needed to play. We only keep what the game needs to work. One necessary cookie, used only when you link an account (its purpose is in section 4), no ads, no trackers, and we sell nothing.

1. Who is responsible

The game is run by SnowRealm (斯諾瑞姆工作室), which is responsible for the data described here. Questions and requests: service@snowrealm.pet. Bug reports can also go to the issue page on GitHub, which is public, so never post a transfer code or password there.

2. What we keep and why

  • A random account id and public tag, to recognise your cloud save. Not linked to who you are.
  • A device key, stored only as a hash that cannot be turned back into the key.
  • Game progress: coins, level, rank, titles, looks and items owned, daily tasks, statistics.
  • A name, if you enter one, shown on leaderboards and room lists. Optional; not available under 13.
  • Leaderboard results, shown publicly.
  • Each match's piece placements, checked against the reported score to stop cheating and then discarded (only the match number is remembered, so it cannot be sent twice).
  • A coin ledger: with a cloud save, the server records each change to your coins (what it was for, how much, the balance after, and when), for support, cheating checks and the game's economy.
  • Members' own pictures and music, if you choose to upload them: only when you pick "Also upload to the cloud" in the member screen or the music player. Kept in Cloudflare R2 (our cloud file storage), visible only to your own account, so you can get them back on another device. By default they stay on your device only. A file you take off the cloud waits in "Recently removed" for 30 days, when you can bring it back, and is then deleted from the cloud. Before upload, each file is encrypted in your browser with AES-256-GCM under its own random key; the keys are kept in 轉磚's database, apart from the files, and everything travels over HTTPS. You can choose your own password instead (PBKDF2, 600,000 rounds): neither it nor the recovery code ever reaches us, so we cannot open those files, and if both are lost they cannot be recovered. Optional email backup: when you set the password you may choose to leave an email address. Its only use is to send you your recovery code when you press "Mail me my recovery code"; no ads, never shared. We first mail a six-digit code to check the address is yours. With an address, 轉磚 keeps a locked copy of the recovery code (its key is in the server settings, not the database), so 轉磚 could technically get it; without one, only you can open your files. Mail is sent through Resend (United States). The address and the kept code stay until you replace the address or delete your cloud save. Once you link a SnowRealm Games account, its address can take this place. Not offered under 13.
  • Direct connection in friends' rooms: when you play a friend through a room code, the two browsers try to connect directly (WebRTC) so moves arrive faster. While connected directly, your friend's browser can see your network (IP) address and you theirs; to find a route, the browser asks a public STUN server (Cloudflare or Google) for your outside address. Random matching never connects directly. Add ?rtc=off to the address to turn it off.
  • A match log: with a cloud save, each match's mode, result, knockouts, score, lines sent, length and whether the placements checked out, plus the room code and player count for online matches. Used for support, cheating reports and game balance.
  • A play log: with a cloud save, one line for each game you play (matches, levels, the adventure, weather challenges, the brick games, the dungeon): which game, mode, result and length. Used to count how many people play each game; the team only sees totals.
  • Error reports: when the keys suddenly stop answering in a match, or the game hits a script error, it sends what it was doing at that moment: the mode, the screen and key state, the screen size, the kind of device (such as "Android Chrome 131") and the error message. No name, save or account. Used to find and fix the fault.
  • Treasure box, wheel and slot records: when each box was opened and what was in it, so the published odds can be checked.
  • Online room messages, deleted once delivered; rooms are removed after three hours at most.
  • A keyed hash of your network address (never the address itself), to slow down abuse; kept two days at most.
  • Ordinary request logs kept by our host (Vercel) under its own terms.

3. What we do not collect

When you play as a guest: no real name, email, phone, birthday, precise location, contacts, photos, advertising ids or payment details (the one exception is an email address you choose to leave to keep your recovery code, below). Pictures you choose for member features stay on your device unless you pick "Also upload to the cloud" (see above). What an account adds is below.

3a. SnowRealm Games accounts (coming soon)

Accounts are optional. Once they launch, linking one adds: the id and email from the service you sign in with (to sign you in, recover the account and send important account notices, never ads); the region you chose as where you live, stored only as a region code, to apply local age rules; only the result of the age check, never your birthday; your friends list and invites; and a record of which guest save was linked to which account and when, to settle disputes. Accounts are run by 斯諾瑞姆工作室 (SnowRealm) and shared across its games; each game receives only what it needs, such as the account number and name. The full account privacy notice will be published at launch.

4. Cookies and storage on your device

The game uses one necessary cookie, set only when you press "Link this save to your SnowRealm Games account" or "Get my save back with SnowRealm Games".

  • Name: __Host-zzauth
  • Purpose: when you come back to 轉磚 after signing in at SnowRealm Games, it checks that the same browser came back, so only you can finish the link and nobody else can use your sign-in
  • Content: a random number, with no name, account or other personal data
  • Lifetime: at most 10 minutes; void once the link is done or the time is up
  • The page cannot read it (HttpOnly), it only travels over encrypted connections (Secure), it is not shared with other sites and is never used for tracking or ads
  • It is needed to finish the link, so it is not asked about separately; if you do not want it, just do not press link, and the game works as usual

This feature is not open yet. Apart from that, it uses the browser's storage (localStorage and IndexedDB) for what the game itself needs: settings, progress, the cloud save identity, match recordings, your first-visit choices, member pictures, and the music player's settings with any music files a member adds (kept on this device and not part of the cloud save; uploaded only if you pick "Also upload to the cloud", see above). Without these the game cannot remember anything, so they are not optional.

Optional: Google Fonts. Only after you allow it (on the first-visit screen or in Settings → Privacy and data) does your browser fetch the game's fonts from Google, which then sees your network address. Without it the game uses your device's fonts. You can change this any time.

4a. Coins for an ad (not switched on yet)

The game has a "coins for an ad" button. It is not switched on yet, and no ad code is loaded. Once it is: an ad plays only when you press the button; it is shown by Google's ad service, so your browser then contacts Google, which handles your network address and device details under its own terms; after the ad Google tells our server directly, and coins are paid only if that message's signature checks out (we keep the transaction id, the time and whether it paid, to never pay twice); there are no ads under 13, only non-personalised ads under 18, and players in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland are asked about personalised ads before any ad loads. This policy will be updated before it is switched on.

5. Services involved

Vercel (hosting; server code runs in Singapore), Neon (database, Singapore), Google Fonts (only if you allow it), GitHub (only if you write to us there). Data may therefore be stored outside your country. We share data with no one else and use none of it for advertising or analytics.

6. How long we keep it

Cloud save and leaderboard results: until you delete them. When you ask to delete, the save is switched off and taken off the leaderboards at once, kept for 30 days (counted on the server's clock; the exact end is shown on screen) so that you can take it back, then deleted for good together with its link to a SnowRealm account. Treasure box records: at least 180 days after each draw, and removed with your cloud data. Match log: one year, then deleted automatically, and removed with your cloud data. Play log: its keeping period will be published here once set, and it is removed with your cloud data. Error reports: not stored in the database, only in the host's run logs, which expire by themselves (about 30 days at most). Coin ledger: as long as the cloud save. Address hashes: two days at most. Rooms: three hours at most. Transfer codes: ten minutes or one use. Device data: until you clear this site's browser data.

7. Your rights

Under Taiwan's Personal Data Protection Act and other laws that may apply to you (such as the EU GDPR) you can: get a copy (Settings → Privacy and data → Download my data); correct your name (Profile); delete (Profile → Cloud save → Delete cloud data: switched off at once, can be taken back for 30 days, then deleted for good, after which only a record of when and how many saves were deleted remains, with nothing that identifies you; clear your browser data for what is on the device); withdraw consent for Google Fonts (Settings); and complain to your data protection authority. As we do not know who you are, we may ask you to make a transfer code in the game to prove a save is yours (put it in your email, never post it publicly).

8. Children and teenagers

On the first visit you pick a language, then where you live and an age band (under 13, 13, 14–15, 16–17, 18 or older). The answers stay on your device, and we never guess where you live from your network address. They are your own answers, not an identity check, and they do not replace a parent's consent or a real-name check. Under 13: no online play, no quick match and no public name, so there is no contact with strangers in the game. 13 to 17: everything is available, and we suggest telling a parent. Parents can ask us to delete a child's data as in section 7.

Creating an account requires the age at which local law lets you consent to the use of your personal data yourself (for example 13 to 16 across EU countries, 13 in the United States, 18 in India). Younger players play as guests and we keep no account data for them. Where local law sets no such age, there is no limit.

9. Players in mainland China

The game holds no online game publishing licence in mainland China and is not operated or promoted there, so it is not connected to the mainland's real-name and anti-addiction system. The Simplified Chinese interface shows the customary age-suitability mark (8+) and healthy-gaming advice. Data is kept in Singapore.

10. Security

All traffic uses HTTPS. Browsers never reach the database directly; its access details exist only in the server's configuration. Keys and transfer codes are stored as hashes. The page runs only our own scripts (Content-Security-Policy). If a breach ever happens we will announce it in the game and notify as the law requires.
