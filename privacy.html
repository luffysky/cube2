1. 誰負責

這個遊戲由斯諾瑞姆工作室（SnowRealm）營運，負責處理本政策所說的資料。有任何問題、要行使權利，請寫信到 service@snowrealm.pet。回報程式錯誤也可以到 GitHub 問題回報頁（那裡是公開的，請不要貼出引繼碼或任何密碼）。

3. 不收集的東西

用路人身分玩時，真實姓名、信箱、電話、生日、精確位置、通訊錄、照片、廣告識別碼、付款資料，我們都不收。會員功能裡你自己選的圖片只存在你的裝置上，不會上傳。綁定帳號會多收的資料見下一段。

3-1. SnowRealm Games 帳號（即將推出）

帳號是選擇性的。推出後，如果你綁定帳號，會多處理這些資料：

  • 登入資料：你用來登入的服務提供的帳號識別碼和信箱，用來讓你登入、找回帳號，以及寄送跟帳號有關的重要通知（不寄廣告）。
  • 你選的居住地區：只存地區代碼，用來套用當地法律規定的年齡和規則。
  • 年齡確認結果：只存「已達當地規定年齡」這個結果，不存你的生日。
  • 好友名單與邀請：你加的好友、送出和收到的邀請。
  • 帳號與路人存檔的綁定紀錄：哪一份存檔綁到哪個帳號、什麼時候綁的，用來處理爭議。

帳號由斯諾瑞姆工作室營運，旗下各遊戲共用；各遊戲只拿到它需要的部分（例如帳號編號和名字）。帳號的完整隱私說明會在推出時一起公布。

4. Cookie 與裝置上的儲存

遊戲不使用 cookie。它用瀏覽器的儲存空間（localStorage、IndexedDB）放遊戲本身需要的東西，沒有這些遊戲就沒辦法記得你的進度和設定，所以不另外詢問：

  • zhuanzhuan.v2：設定、鍵位、道場進度
  • zhuanzhuan.meta.v1：遊戲進度的副本
  • zhuanzhuan.cloud.v1：雲端存檔的身分和還沒送出的操作
  • zhuanzhuan.rep.v1：對戰錄影（分身對戰用）
  • zhuanzhuan.consent.v1：你在第一次進來時的選擇
  • zhuanzhuan-pics（IndexedDB）：會員自己選的圖片

選擇性的：Google 字型。遊戲的字型放在 Google Fonts。只有你在第一次進來的畫面或「設定 → 隱私與資料」允許之後，瀏覽器才會連到 Google 下載字型（Google 會因此看到你的網路位址）。不允許也能完整玩，只是用裝置內建的字型。隨時可以改。

4-1. 看廣告領金幣（尚未開通）

遊戲裡有「看廣告領金幣」，目前還沒開通，也不會載入任何廣告程式。開通後的做法：

  • 只有你在「福利」按下按鈕才會播廣告，不會強迫你看，也不會在遊戲中途跳出來。
  • 廣告由 Google 的廣告服務播放。按下去之後，你的瀏覽器才會連到 Google，Google 會依它的規則處理你的網路位址、裝置資訊等資料。
  • 看完之後，Google 直接通知我們的伺服器，伺服器確認簽章才發金幣。我們只記交易編號、時間和有沒有發，用來避免重複發放。
  • 未滿 13 歲不顯示廣告。未滿 18 歲只用非個人化廣告。歐洲經濟區、英國和瑞士的玩家，在載入廣告前會先詢問同不同意個人化廣告。
  • 開通前本政策會更新，詳細說明會一起公布。

5. 經手的服務

  • Vercel：網站和伺服器程式的主機，伺服器程式在新加坡區域執行。
  • Neon：資料庫，位於新加坡。
  • Google Fonts：只在你允許時使用。
  • Google 廣告服務：只在「看廣告領金幣」開通、而且你自己按下去時使用（目前未開通）。
  • GitHub：只在你自己到問題回報頁留言時。

這些服務依照各自的條款處理資料，可能把資料存放在你所在地以外的國家（例如新加坡、美國）。我們不把資料提供給其他任何人，也不拿去做廣告或分析。

6. 保存多久

  • 雲端存檔與排行榜成績：直到你刪除。你按下刪除後立刻停用、不再公開，保留 30 天後永久刪除。
  • 帳號資料：直到你刪除帳號；綁定紀錄在刪除轉磚資料或帳號時一起停用，30 天後跟著存檔永久刪除。
  • 寶箱、轉盤、拉霸紀錄：至少保存到該次開箱後 180 天，之後可能刪除；你刪除雲端資料時一併在 30 天後刪除。
  • 對局紀錄：一年，之後自動刪除；你刪除雲端資料時一併在 30 天後刪除。
  • 金幣流水帳：跟雲端存檔一樣，直到你刪除；刪除時一併在 30 天後永久刪除。
  • 網路位址的雜湊值：最久兩天。
  • 連線房間：最久三小時；訊息讀過就刪。
  • 引繼碼：10 分鐘或用過一次就失效。
  • 裝置上的資料：直到你清除這個網站的瀏覽器資料。

7. 你的權利

依照中華民國《個人資料保護法》，以及你所在地可能適用的法律（例如歐盟 GDPR），你可以：

  • 查詢、取得副本：「設定 → 隱私與資料 → 下載我的資料」會給你一份 JSON 檔。
  • 更正：在「個人檔案」改名字。
  • 刪除：「個人檔案 → 雲端存檔 → 刪除雲端資料」，存檔、排行榜成績、對局紀錄、金幣流水帳和寶箱紀錄立刻停用並從排行榜移除，從按下確定的那一刻起保留 30 天（以伺服器時間計算，畫面會顯示確切的截止時間），期間不能再改動或遊玩這份雲端存檔，但可以在同一處按「取消刪除」恢復，或之後用綁定的 SnowRealm 帳號登入恢復；期滿後永久刪除，SnowRealm 綁定紀錄也一起刪除。刪除完成後只留下「哪個時間刪了幾份」的紀錄，不含任何可以認出你的資料。刪除轉磚的雲端資料不會刪掉 SnowRealm Games 帳號或其他遊戲的資料；裝置上的資料清除瀏覽器資料即可。有帳號時，可以只刪轉磚的資料，或刪除整個 SnowRealm Games 帳號（所有遊戲的資料一起刪）。
  • 撤回同意：Google 字型隨時可以在設定裡關掉。
  • 申訴：可以先聯絡我們；你也有權向所在地的個資主管機關申訴。

因為我們不知道你是誰，寫信提出要求時，我們可能需要你在遊戲裡產生一組引繼碼、寫在信裡，來確認存檔是你的（不要公開貼出）。

8. 兒童與青少年

第一次進來時會先讓你選語言，再自己選居住地區和年齡區間（未滿 13、13、14–15、16–17、18 歲以上），答案只存在你的裝置上，我們不會用網路位置推測你住在哪裡。這是你自己的回答，不是身分驗證，也不能代替家長同意或實名認證。未滿 13 歲：不開放連線對戰和隨機配對，也不能設定公開的名字，這樣就不會在遊戲裡接觸到陌生人，排行榜上也只會出現代號。13 到 17 歲可以使用全部功能，我們建議先告訴家長。家長如果認為孩子的資料需要刪除，請照第 7 點聯絡我們。

註冊帳號要達到你所在地法律規定、可以自己同意提供個人資料的年齡（例如歐盟各國 13 到 16 歲、美國 13 歲、印度 18 歲）；未達的玩家只能用路人身分玩，我們不收他們的帳號資料。當地法律沒有這類規定的，就不限年齡。

9. 中國大陸的玩家

本遊戲未在中國大陸取得網路遊戲出版許可，也沒有在中國大陸營運或刻意向中國大陸推廣，因此沒有接上中國大陸的實名認證與防沉迷系統。簡體中文介面依當地習慣標示適齡提示（8+）與健康遊戲忠告。資料存放在新加坡。

10. 安全

所有連線都經過 HTTPS。瀏覽器從來不直接連資料庫；資料庫的連線資訊只存在伺服器的環境設定裡。密鑰和引繼碼都只存雜湊值。頁面只執行我們自己的程式（Content-Security-Policy）。沒有任何系統是絕對安全的，萬一發生資料外洩，我們會在遊戲裡公告並依法通知。

11. 變更與聯絡

政策有變更時，上面的日期會跟著改；重大變更會在遊戲裡告知。聯絡我們：service@snowrealm.pet。另見服務條款。

Privacy policy

Zhuan Zhuan Battle · SnowRealm (斯諾瑞姆工作室) · updated 20 November 2026

No sign-up and no email are needed to play. We only keep what the game needs to work. No cookies, no ads, no trackers, and we sell nothing.

1. Who is responsible

The game is run by SnowRealm (斯諾瑞姆工作室), which is responsible for the data described here. Questions and requests: service@snowrealm.pet. Bug reports can also go to the issue page on GitHub, which is public, so never post a transfer code or password there.

2. What we keep and why

  • A random account id and public tag, to recognise your cloud save. Not linked to who you are.
  • A device key, stored only as a hash that cannot be turned back into the key.
  • Game progress: coins, level, rank, titles, looks and items owned, daily tasks, statistics.
  • A name, if you enter one, shown on leaderboards and room lists. Optional; not available under 13.
  • Leaderboard results, shown publicly.
  • Each match's piece placements, checked against the reported score to stop cheating and then discarded (only the match number is remembered, so it cannot be sent twice).
  • A coin ledger: with a cloud save, the server records each change to your coins (what it was for, how much, the balance after, and when), for support, cheating checks and the game's economy.
  • A match log: with a cloud save, each match's mode, result, knockouts, score, lines sent, length and whether the placements checked out, plus the room code and player count for online matches. Used for support, cheating reports and game balance.
  • Treasure box, wheel and slot records: when each box was opened and what was in it, so the published odds can be checked.
  • Online room messages, deleted once delivered; rooms are removed after three hours at most.
  • A keyed hash of your network address (never the address itself), to slow down abuse; kept two days at most.
  • Ordinary request logs kept by our host (Vercel) under its own terms.

3. What we do not collect

When you play as a guest: no real name, email, phone, birthday, precise location, contacts, photos, advertising ids or payment details. Pictures you choose for member features stay on your device. What an account adds is below.

3a. SnowRealm Games accounts (coming soon)

Accounts are optional. Once they launch, linking one adds: the id and email from the service you sign in with (to sign you in, recover the account and send important account notices, never ads); the region you chose as where you live, stored only as a region code, to apply local age rules; only the result of the age check, never your birthday; your friends list and invites; and a record of which guest save was linked to which account and when, to settle disputes. Accounts are run by 斯諾瑞姆工作室 (SnowRealm) and shared across its games; each game receives only what it needs, such as the account number and name. The full account privacy notice will be published at launch.

4. Cookies and storage on your device

The game uses no cookies. It uses the browser's storage (localStorage and IndexedDB) for what the game itself needs: settings, progress, the cloud save identity, match recordings, your first-visit choices and member pictures. Without these the game cannot remember anything, so they are not optional.

Optional: Google Fonts. Only after you allow it (on the first-visit screen or in Settings → Privacy and data) does your browser fetch the game's fonts from Google, which then sees your network address. Without it the game uses your device's fonts. You can change this any time.

4a. Coins for an ad (not switched on yet)

The game has a "coins for an ad" button. It is not switched on yet, and no ad code is loaded. Once it is: an ad plays only when you press the button; it is shown by Google's ad service, so your browser then contacts Google, which handles your network address and device details under its own terms; after the ad Google tells our server directly, and coins are paid only if that message's signature checks out (we keep the transaction id, the time and whether it paid, to never pay twice); there are no ads under 13, only non-personalised ads under 18, and players in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland are asked about personalised ads before any ad loads. This policy will be updated before it is switched on.

5. Services involved

Vercel (hosting; server code runs in Singapore), Neon (database, Singapore), Google Fonts (only if you allow it), GitHub (only if you write to us there). Data may therefore be stored outside your country. We share data with no one else and use none of it for advertising or analytics.

6. How long we keep it

Cloud save and leaderboard results: until you delete them. Account data: until you delete the account; link records go with this game's data or the account. Treasure box records: at least 180 days after each draw, removed with your cloud data. Match log: one year, then deleted automatically, and removed with your cloud data. Coin ledger: as long as the cloud save, until you delete it. Address hashes: two days at most. Rooms: three hours at most. Transfer codes: ten minutes or one use. Device data: until you clear this site's browser data.

7. Your rights

Under Taiwan's Personal Data Protection Act and other laws that may apply to you (such as the EU GDPR) you can: get a copy (Settings → Privacy and data → Download my data); correct your name (Profile); delete everything (Profile → Cloud save → Delete cloud data, and clear your browser data; with an account, delete only this game's data or the whole SnowRealm Games account); withdraw consent for Google Fonts (Settings); and complain to your data protection authority. As we do not know who you are, we may ask you to make a transfer code in the game to prove a save is yours (put it in your email, never post it publicly).

8. Children and teenagers

On the first visit you pick a language, then where you live and an age band (under 13, 13, 14–15, 16–17, 18 or older). The answers stay on your device, and we never guess where you live from your network address. They are your own answers, not an identity check, and they do not replace a parent's consent or a real-name check. Under 13: no online play, no quick match and no public name, so there is no contact with strangers in the game. 13 to 17: everything is available, and we suggest telling a parent. Parents can ask us to delete a child's data as in section 7.

Creating an account requires the age at which local law lets you consent to the use of your personal data yourself (for example 13 to 16 across EU countries, 13 in the United States, 18 in India). Younger players play as guests and we keep no account data for them. Where local law sets no such age, there is no limit.

9. Players in mainland China

The game holds no online game publishing licence in mainland China and is not operated or promoted there, so it is not connected to the mainland's real-name and anti-addiction system. The Simplified Chinese interface shows the customary age-suitability mark (8+) and healthy-gaming advice. Data is kept in Singapore.

10. Security

All traffic uses HTTPS. Browsers never reach the database directly; its access details exist only in the server's configuration. Keys and transfer codes are stored as hashes. The page runs only our own scripts (Content-Security-Policy). If a breach ever happens we will announce it in the game and notify as the law requires.

11. Changes and contact

The date above changes whenever this policy does; important changes are announced in the game. Contact: service@snowrealm.pet. See also the terms of service.
