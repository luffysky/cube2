隱私說明

轉磚對戰 · SnowRealm Games · 2026 年 11 月 19 日版

不用註冊、不用信箱就能玩。遊戲沒有廣告，沒有追蹤程式，也不使用 cookie。下面是它實際存了什麼。

只留在你裝置上的

  • 設定、鍵位、道場進度、對戰錄影：存在瀏覽器的儲存空間。
  • 會員功能裡你自己選的圖片：只存在你的裝置，不會上傳。

清除這個網站的瀏覽器資料，就全部刪掉了。

存在伺服器上的（雲端存檔）

你第一次打完一場、買東西或領獎勵時，遊戲會自動建立一個匿名的雲端存檔，讓進度不會因為換裝置或清瀏覽器而消失。

隨機產生的帳號編號和公開代號用來認得這份存檔。跟你的真實身分沒有關聯。
裝置密鑰裝置自己留著原文，伺服器只存它的雜湊值，沒辦法還原。
遊戲進度金幣、段位、買過的外觀和道具、每日任務、統計數字。
名字你自己填的才有，會顯示在排行榜上。可以留空。
排行榜成績段位分、單場最高分、40 行競速時間。排行榜是公開的。
連線對戰的房間房間代碼和雙方互傳的遊戲訊息。對方收到就刪除，房間最久三小時後清掉。
網路位址不存原文。只在防止濫用（例如一直猜引繼碼）時，暫存一個無法還原的雜湊值，最久兩天後刪除。

伺服器不知道你的姓名、信箱、電話或位置，遊戲也沒有向你要這些。

經手的服務

  • 網站和伺服器程式放在 Vercel，資料庫放在 Neon（新加坡）。它們依照各自的條款保留一般的連線紀錄。
  • 字型從 Google Fonts 載入，所以你的瀏覽器會向 Google 連線取得字型檔。

我們不會把資料賣給別人，也不拿去做廣告。

怎麼刪除

  • 雲端資料：主選單的「個人檔案」→「雲端存檔」→「刪除雲端資料」。存檔和排行榜成績會立刻刪掉，救不回來。
  • 裝置上的資料：清除這個網站的瀏覽器資料。

名字的規則

排行榜上的名字不能是辱罵字眼，也不能冒充官方或管理員。不合規定的名字會被清空，改顯示公開代號。

有問題

到 GitHub 上的問題回報頁留言。這份說明有更動時，上面的日期會跟著改。

Privacy

Zhuan Zhuan Battle · SnowRealm Games · 19 November 2026

You can play without signing up and without an email address. The game has no ads, no trackers and no cookies. This is what it stores.

Kept on your device only

  • Settings, key bindings, dojo progress and match recordings, in the browser's storage.
  • Pictures you choose for the member features. They stay on your device and are never uploaded.

Clearing this site's browser data removes all of it.

Kept on the server (cloud save)

The first time you finish a match, buy something or claim a reward, the game creates an anonymous cloud save so your progress survives a new device or a cleared browser.

A random account id and a public tagThey identify the save and are not linked to who you are.
A device keyYour device keeps it. The server stores only a hash that cannot be turned back into the key.
Game progressCoins, rank, the looks and items you own, daily tasks, play statistics.
NameOnly if you enter one. It is shown on the leaderboards. You can leave it empty.
Leaderboard resultsRank points, best single-match score, 40-line sprint time. The leaderboards are public.
Online match roomsThe room code and the game messages the two players exchange. A message is deleted once delivered, and a room is removed after three hours at most.
Network addressNever stored as it is. To slow down abuse, a hash that cannot be reversed is kept for two days at most.

The server does not know your real name, email, phone number or location, and the game does not ask for them.

Services involved

  • The site and its server code run on Vercel, and the database is hosted by Neon in Singapore. Both keep ordinary connection logs under their own terms.
  • Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google to fetch them.

We do not sell this data or use it for advertising.

Deleting your data

  • Cloud data: Profile → Cloud save → Delete cloud data, from the main menu. The save and your leaderboard results are removed at once and cannot be restored.
  • Data on your device: clear this site's browser data.

Names

A leaderboard name may not be an insult and may not pose as staff. A name that breaks this is cleared, and your public tag is shown instead.
